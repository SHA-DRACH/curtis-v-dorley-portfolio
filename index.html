About

From community organiser to national policymaker

Hon. Curtis V. Dorley was nominated by President Joseph Nyumah Boakai Sr. on 1 February 2024 as Deputy Minister for Research, Policy and Planning at the Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection (MGCSP). He serves alongside Minister Gbeme Horace-Kollie and oversees the ministry's policy development, research agenda and planning.

His public service began at the community level. During the 2014 Ebola outbreak, as Board Chairman of the Karney Foundation for Youth Empowerment, Advocacy and Transparency, he led an awareness campaign that reached about 7,000 people in Walapolu Chiefdom, Bong County. He went on to chair the Unity Party's Bong County Youth Wing and later its National Youth Wing.

At the ministry he has led the rollout of Gender and Social Inclusion Units across government, trained pilot ministries in Gender Responsive Planning and Budgeting, and steered the validation of Liberia's revised National Gender Policy.

Key areas of work

What he is building at the ministry

National Gender Policy 2026–2036

Led the regional validation of Liberia's revised ten-year policy in Gbarnga, July 2026: the national blueprint for gender equality, social inclusion and women's empowerment.

Gender & Social Inclusion Units

Championed GSIUs in every ministry and agency, each with three to seven staff, and opened their first national coordination meeting in September 2024.

Gender Responsive Planning & Budgeting

Led GRPB training for nine pilot spending entities, including the Ministries of Agriculture, Health, Justice, Education and Internal Affairs.

Targets to 2029

Briefed senior management on the ministry's national targets to 2029 in gender equality, child protection, social protection and reducing gender-based violence.

Research partnerships

Strengthened ties with the University of Liberia's College of Gender Studies and Interdisciplinary Research, including seconded staff and support for gender statistics.

Survivor-centred justice

Represented the ministry at the ADWANGA justice conference, October 2026, calling for responses to conflict-related sexual violence that put survivors first.
